# Project identity

**Name:** 5imic — Borrowed Voice Studio

**Tagline:** The voice is the sample.

**GitHub short description (ready to paste):**

> A local-first voice collage instrument: capture permitted speech, cut real clips, and stitch new lines for any game or call.

**Suggested GitHub topics:**

`audio-dsp` · `concatenative-synthesis` · `offline-speech-recognition` · `virtual-microphone` · `voice-tools` · `windows` · `linux`

**Project pitch:**

5imic turns a live conversation into a strange, playable instrument. It listens
to permitted audio, stores short real word and phrase recordings, and uses them
to construct entirely new sentences without a generative voice clone. A tiny
pinnable input bar, unified terminal console, and direct virtual microphone
output make it usable while playing games or talking online. The sound keeps
the original grain, pitch, cadence, and imperfections of the recordings.

Every missing word is visible. Every clip is local. You control the recording.

**Positioning:** experimental open-source voice instrument; not a polished
production voice changer or a guarantee of real-time natural speech.
